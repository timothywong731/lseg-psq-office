BEHIND THE SPACE

Built from a
different perspective.

A visual reconstruction of the Paternoster Square office, informed by all 28 reference images and three walkthrough videos, including the September 2026 visit.

The tapered atrium, launch balcony, reception and six-lift lobbies were modelled in Blender and brought to the browser with Three.js. The launch ceremony includes animated displays, confetti, clapping and synthesized crowd sound.

A spatial interpretation

Dimensions and materials are estimated from the references, not a measured survey. Market graphics are illustrative, not live data. This independent study is not affiliated with or endorsed by LSEG.

People: Man by Quaternius (CC0). Chair: Low Poly Office Chair by NoodleBaguette, CC BY 4.0; scaled and textures resized.
